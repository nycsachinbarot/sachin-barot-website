Why this exists

A space our community rarely gets.

In our community, mental health is rarely talked about.

Many of us carry inherited stories and unspoken rules, from childhood and from the generations before us.

This is a space to set some of that down, to reconnect with our ancestors, our culture, and our music, and to be among people who understand without needing it explained.

What the days look like

Slow mornings. Full hearts.

  • Daily yoga

    Gentle, all levels.

  • Pranayama & breathwork

    Breath as a way back to calm.

  • Morning workout

    Move your body as the day begins.

  • Fresh, healthy food

    Shared meals, made with care.

  • Time in nature

    Sea, jungle, and open sky.

  • The Heart Method

    Guided group inner work.

  • Singing & dancing

    The music we grew up with.

  • Fireside chats

    Real conversation, after dark.

Your host

Sachin Barot

Sachin is South Asian and a former Wall Street executive. After leaving that world, he spent several years on his own healing, investing in the mental wellness space and building his own practice.

He is a trained meditator, grounded in Vipassana and the Tergar Meditation Teacher Program. Today he hosts small retreats for people who want to slow down and reconnect.

He's building the kind of space he once went looking for, and couldn't find.
